# Provider logo assets

The provider marks in this directory are shown only to identify the
corresponding third-party CLI or service that NexusIDE can operate. All
trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. They imply no
affiliation with or endorsement by those owners.

The files come from the NexusIDE application
(`src/renderer/assets/provider-logos/`), which records their origin:

- `claude.svg`, `codex.svg`, `kimi.svg`, `grok.svg`, `glm.svg`, `longcat.svg`,
  `agy.svg`, `opencode.svg` and `gemini-color.svg` were sourced from
  `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.91.0 (MIT), without changing their geometry.
  On this site `longcat.svg` replaces `currentColor` with a light fill so the
  mark stays visible on the dark page.
- `deepseek-color.svg`, `minimax-code.svg` and `qwen-color.svg` are vendor
  marks from the same lobehub icon set, as shipped in NexusIDE.
- `aider.png` is the public avatar of the official `Aider-AI` GitHub
  organization. `mimo-code.png` is the mark used by NexusIDE for MiMo Code.
- `muse.svg`, `openclaude.svg`, `devin.svg` and `cursor-agent.svg` are original
  geometric identifiers drawn for NexusIDE, not vendor artwork. They reproduce
  no part of any official logo and borrow only the vendor's accent colour.
