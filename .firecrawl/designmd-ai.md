## How to use DESIGN.md in your project

Three steps. No setup. Works with any AI coding tool.

1

### Download a DESIGN.md

Browse the library and download any design system as a single `DESIGN.md` file.

2

### Drop it in your project

Drag the file into the root of your repo — next to your `README.md`. That's the whole install.

3

### Ask your AI to use it

Tell Claude, Cursor, or any AI coding tool: "Use the @DESIGN.md file and style my app."
